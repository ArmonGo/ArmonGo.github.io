A collection of oil pastel paintings from the past few years.

Gathering the photos took a while — I was surprised by how many I had actually made. After moving, I hadn't seen most of them and assumed they were lost. Then I realized my friends had "robbed" me over the years, quietly taking pieces home from my studio :).

I hope they're all doing well in their new homes.
