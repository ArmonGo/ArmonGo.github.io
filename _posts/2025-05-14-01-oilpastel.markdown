A collection of oil pastel paintings I made over the past few years.

It took me a while to gather all these photos. I was ultimately surprised by how many of these I had actually done.

Ever since I moved, I hadn't seen many of these.

At first, I thought I had simply lost many of them. Though quickly after I realized that my friends had "robbed" me and taken them (meaning: took from my storage/atelier/class) :)!

I hope they're all doing well in their homes.
